Global Eclipse Patagonia Gathering 2020

Global Eclipse Patagonia Gathering was an event done Neuquén, Argentina. It was an event to commemorate a total Lunar/Sun Eclipse. Full of art, music, educational workshops, yoga, dance, permaculture and myriad opportunities to inspire the soul and enliven the spirit.
