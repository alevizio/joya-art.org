Joya Crystallized

Masaru Emoto, a Japanese researcher, discovered that human emotions can influence the molecular structure of water, which makes up about 70% of the human body. Using high-speed photography, he showed that water exposed to positive thoughts and words forms beautiful, intricate crystals, while water subjected to negative influences forms incomplete, dull crystals.

Building on Emoto's findings, the "Joya Crystallized" experience involves participants gathering to charge water with positive intentions. This water, placed inside a jewel-like container and labeled with affirmations such as "love" and "peace," is then consumed by participants, who symbolically absorb these positive messages. Some days later the participants receive an image of a crystal of our charged water as a memento of the encounter.

Emoto's work suggests that positive human thoughts and words can significantly impact our health and the environment, advocating for a conscious approach to our interactions with water and each other.
